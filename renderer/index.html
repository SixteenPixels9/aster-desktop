How sound waves work

Sound is a moving pattern of pressure. The air vibrates locally while the pattern travels outward.

1. Source vibratesA speaker cone moves.
→
2. Air compressesMolecules crowd and spread.
→
3. Pattern travelsEach layer nudges the next.
→
4. Ear receives itYour eardrum vibrates.

Worked example

A speaker playing 440 Hz vibrates 440 times each second. Double it to 880 Hz and the note sounds one octave higher.
